Straight answer

Will this actually fix my car?

Computers get blamed far more often than they fail — it is usually a sensor, a wire or a ground. Here is what a clone fixes and what it doesn't, so you don't pay us for something that won't help.

A clone fixes

  • A computer a shop or scan tool has diagnosed as failed
  • Water, heat or voltage damage, as long as the memory still reads
  • A used computer that won't start the car because of anti-theft
  • A discontinued module with no new part left to buy

A clone won't fix

  • A no-start caused by a sensor, fuel, wiring or a bad ground
  • A computer so dead it can't be read — we tell you before you pay
  • Adding new keys, or resetting anti-theft on the car itself
  • A donor that isn't the same part number as your original

Before you pull it

Common questions

Can I just buy a used computer and plug it in?

No, and this is the trap almost everyone falls into. The anti-theft key lives inside the computer, locked to the car it came from. A junkyard unit typically cranks, runs about two seconds and dies — six times, then it disables the starter. Cloning your original onto that unit is the fix.

Will my keys still work?

Yes. Your immobilizer and key data are part of what gets copied, so the keys in your pocket keep working. No dealer visit, no re-cutting, no re-learning.

What if my original is completely dead?

If the board won't power up we can often still read the memory chip directly and build the twin from that. If even that fails, we tell you first and go over your options with you before anything else happens.

Do you work with repair shops?

Yes, at the same price. Send several modules in one box and pay one return charge. Call the number above to open a trade line.

What if I don't have a donor?

That's Path B, and it's the part no other shop does. Ship only your original and we source a good used, part-number-matched module for $299 all-in. For common families we usually have one in 2 to 5 days.

Is cloning legal?

Yes. You are restoring your own vehicle's data onto a replacement part for your own car, which is why we ask for the VIN and proof the vehicle is yours before we start.

How long does it take?

One business day from the day your computer arrives. Units received by midday ship the same business day; everything else goes out the next.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, on modules we supply — when TwinECM sources the twin under Path B, that module is covered for 12 months. A donor you supply yourself isn't covered, since we can't vouch for where it came from.
