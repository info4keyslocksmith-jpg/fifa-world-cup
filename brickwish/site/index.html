    Questions

    Can my child really ask for anything?

    Yes. Buildings, bridges, mountains, parks, animals, vehicles, dreams. Brand characters become a look-alike of our own, and anything scary becomes a friendly version. We tell you when we change something.

    Can we see it before we pay?

    Yes. You see your child's model building itself and the first 5 pages of its manual before you pay.

    How long does it take?

    The design and preview are ready in a few minutes. The bricks arrive in about 1–2 weeks.

    What if a piece is missing?

    Every kit has spare small pieces. If something is still missing, we mail it free.

    Do the bricks work with other brands?

    Yes. Our bricks are compatible with LEGO® bricks and most other major building-brick brands.
