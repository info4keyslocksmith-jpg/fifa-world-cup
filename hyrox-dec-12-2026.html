What you're training for

Where the race is actually won

  • Wall balls — the last station, 100 reps, and the single biggest spread between athletes. A 6-minute wall ball set and a 12-minute one both happen at every race. This is why they appear in training from Week 3 onward, always on tired legs.
  • Sled push — the great equaliser. It's a leg-strength and body-angle problem, not a cardio one, and it wrecks your next 1 km run if you go into it cold. Trained heavy every Monday.
  • Roxzone — free time for anyone who practises it. Don't walk transitions in training; jog them, always.
  • Compromised running — running fast is not the skill. Running fast after 100 wall balls is. That's the entire point of Thursdays and the Saturday simulations.

This structure repeats for all 15 weeks. What changes week to week is volume, intensity and which specific session lands on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday — that's the table in §07.

If a week goes sideways and you can only train three days: keep Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Those three carry the block. Strength is the first thing to cut, the long session is the last.

06

Pool & sauna — the recovery engine

Pool P-1 / P-2

Two uses, alternating through the block.

  • P-1 · Recovery swim (Phase 1, deloads, race week) — 25–30 min easy, mix strokes, nothing above conversational. Flushes the legs after Monday and Tuesday.
  • P-2 · Aerobic intervals (Phases 2–4) — 400 m easy warm-up, then 10 × 50 m moderate on 1:15, then 200 m easy. Real aerobic stimulus with none of the pounding — this is how the running volume in weeks 9–11 stays survivable.
  • Bonus: breath control in the water transfers directly to wall-ball pacing. Practise steady exhale on effort.

Sauna SA

Heat adaptation expands plasma volume over ~3 consistent weeks — worth having, because indoor Hyrox venues run hot and humid even in December.

  • Dose: 15–20 min, post-session, 3× per week. Never before a hard session.
  • Priority window: weeks 9–13. That's when it pays off for race day.
  • Rehydrate: 500 ml + electrolytes after every sitting. Weigh in/out if you want to be exact.
  • Race week: one short easy sitting Monday, then stop. Don't add heat stress to a taper.
  • Skip it entirely if you're ill, or if HRV has been trending down for several days.

Estimate max HR as 208 − (0.7 × age), or use the highest figure Health has ever recorded for you.

  • Z2 60–70% — all easy running. Conversational. Most of your volume.
  • Z3 70–80% — steady, race-effort running.
  • Z4 80–90% — threshold and 1 km repeats.
  • Z5 90%+ — 400s and station finishes only.
08

Session library

Every code in the table above, written out. Warm-up for all sessions: 10 min easy movement, leg swings, 3 × 20 m build-ups.

R-1

Z2 base run

  • Continuous easy running at Z2 pace from §04.
  • Nose-breathing test: if you can't, you're going too fast.
  • Duration set by the week's volume target.
R-2

400 m repeats

  • 8–16 × 400 m at 400 pace, jog recovery 90s falling to 60s across the block.
  • Even splits beat a fast first rep. Last one should be your quickest.
R-3

1 km repeats @ threshold

  • 4–6 × 1 km at threshold pace, 90s standing or walking rest.
  • Controlled discomfort, not a race. You should finish able to do one more.
R-4

Hyrox pace repeats

  • 6–8 × 1 km at race pace, 45–60s rest.
  • The single most specific running session in the plan. From Week 9, this is your Tuesday.
  • Variant: 20 wall balls in each rest period.
S-A

Strength A — push & sled

  • Back squat or trap-bar deadlift — see progression in §07.
  • DB shoulder press 3 × 10.
  • Sled push 6 × 20 m heavy (above race load), full recovery.
  • Wall balls 5 × 20, 60s rest.
  • Core: 3 × 45s front plank + 3 × 12 hanging knee raise.
  • Finish: 15 min compromised run straight off the last set.
S-B

Strength B — pull, posterior & grip

  • Deadlift or RDL — see progression.
  • Weighted pull-ups 4 × 5.
  • Sled pull 6 × 25 m at race load — practise the hand-over-hand rhythm.
  • Farmers carry 4 × 50 m heavy, no drops.
  • Sandbag lunges 4 × 25 m.
  • Grip finisher: 3 × max-hold bar hang.
H-1

Station circuit

  • All 8 stations in race order at 50–70% of race volume (e.g. 500 m ski, 25 m sled, 40 m BBJ, 50 wall balls).
  • Jog every transition — never walk. That's Roxzone practice.
  • Technique day: fix your sled body angle, wall-ball catch depth, lunge step length.
H-2

Compromised doubles

  • 4–6 rounds: 1 km run at race pace → one full station at race volume → jog transition.
  • Rotate which stations you use so all 8 get covered across the phase.
  • The whole point: teaching your legs to run after load.
H-3

Half simulation

  • 4 × 1 km + 4 stations, race order, race loads, clock running throughout.
  • Record every split. Compare against your split budget in §03.
  • Rehearse race nutrition and kit here, not on race day.
H-4

Full simulation

  • The whole race, timed, full loads, jogged transitions. Week 13, Saturday.
  • Treat it as a dress rehearsal: same breakfast, same shoes, same fuelling as race day.
  • Expect to be flat for 3–4 days afterwards — Week 14 is built for that.
P-1 / P-2 / SA

Pool & sauna

  • Full protocol in §06.
  • P-1 recovery swim · P-2 aerobic intervals · SA 15–20 min heat.
09

Race day · Saturday 12 December

The 24 hours before
  • Nothing new. Not shoes, not food, not supplements.
  • Normal carbs, extra fluid, salt with dinner.
  • Lay kit out the night before. Bring your own chalk and a small towel.
  • Breakfast 2.5–3 h before your start — the same one you rehearsed in Week 13.
Warm-up
  • 15 min easy jog, finishing ~25 min before your slot.
  • Leg swings, 3 × 20 m build-ups, 10 air squats, 10 easy wall balls.
  • Stay warm. Venues are cold in the corral and hot on the floor.

Pacing the thing

  • Run 1 will feel absurdly easy. That's correct. Everyone goes out 30–40s/km too hot and pays for it at the sandbag lunges. Hold your split-budget pace.
  • Sled push: low body angle, short choppy steps, don't stop moving. Stopping costs more than slowing down.
  • Burpee broad jumps: pick a rhythm you can hold for the full 80 m and never break it. Slow and unbroken beats fast and stuttering.
  • Wall balls: plan the breaks before you start — sets of 10 with 3 breaths, or 25/25/25/25. Deciding mid-set is how a 7-minute station becomes 12.
  • Roxzone: jog every transition, even the last one. You practised it every Thursday.
